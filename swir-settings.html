Language & Region

Global BCP-47 locale used by the shell and portable SWIR applications. Any standards-valid locale is accepted; translation packs can be installed independently.

Examples: pl-PLnb-NOen-USar-SAzh-Hans-CN. Locale controls language preference, formatting and LTR/RTL direction. Missing strings fall back safely to the base language and then English.
